How to play

No studying, no wrong answers — just a reason to talk, laugh and learn something new.

01

Roll & move

Teams take turns rolling the dice and moving their pawn along either route. Safe is longer; Risk is shorter and spicier.

02

Take the prompt

Land on a space to reveal a challenge, object or special action. Complete it together before the next turn.

03

Keep your hearts

Every team begins with three hearts. Gain and lose lives, but never go above three or below zero.

04

Reach the finish

Make it to the finish with hearts to win. If a team reaches zero hearts, the other team wins.
